## Brand & Style

This design system expresses clarity, cognitive velocity, and uncompromising precision. Crafted for professionals, founders, and knowledge workers who rely on automated intelligence to craft flawless communication, the aesthetic pairs the rigor of enterprise software with the approachability of modern productivity tools.

### Visual Aesthetic & Movement
The design language sits at the intersection of **Modern Minimalist SaaS** and **Tactile Utility**. Surfaces are luminous, airy, and purposefully organized to reduce cognitive overhead during high-speed drafting. Visual interest is driven by clean micro-borders, atmospheric indigo-tinted ambient shadows, and crisp dual-accent energetic highlights rather than heavy ornamental chrome.

### Emotional Goals
- **Empowered & Frictionless:** Interacting with generation tools feels instantaneous and effortless.
- **Authoritative & Trustworthy:** AI suggestions, rewrite options, and structural adjustments project editorial expertise without being pushy.
- **Calm Focus:** Pristine canvas spaces isolate the drafted word, ensuring high legibility and deep focus.

## Colors

The color architecture is calibrated for long reading sessions and precise inline editorial markup, meeting strict WCAG AA contrast ratios across all states.

### Foundation & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base (`#FAFAFC`):** Soft, warm-tinted off-white reducing eye strain on OLED and standard screens.
- **Surface Layer 1 (`#FFFFFF`):** High-clarity white for content cards, text editors, and floating inspect panels.
- **Surface Layer 2 (`#F1F5F9`):** Subdued slate foundation used for sidebars, toolbar trays, and inactive button states.
- **Border Default (`#E2E8F0`):** Ultra-clean 1px structural division across cards and controls.
- **Border Focus (`#93C5FD`):** Accent ring for active editors and focused controls.

### Text & Ink Hierarchy
- **Text Primary (`#0F172A`):** Deep navy-charcoal for principal headlines, active copy, and core interactions.
- **Text Secondary (`#475569`):** Balanced slate for metadata, supporting descriptions, and label prompts.
- **Text Subtle (`#94A3B8`):** Restrained slate for placeholders, keyboard shortcuts, and disabled counts.

### Accents & Gradients
- **Primary Energy (`#2563EB`):** High-confidence electric blue representing proactive intelligence and primary actions.
- **Secondary Flow (`#0284C7`):** Vivid sky cyan for secondary actions, interactive tabs, and selection states.
- **Tertiary Utility (`#0D9488`):** Deep teal for tone-adjustment tools and readability enhancements.
- **AI Spark Gradient:** `linear-gradient(135deg, #2563EB 0%, #0284C7 50%, #0D9488 100%)` applied sparingly to AI trigger buttons, premium badges, and generation progress indicators.

### Editorial & Semantic Diagnostics
- **Success & Clarity (`#059669` / Soft Surface `#ECFDF5`):** Denotes optimized readability score, accepted rewrite, and valid syntax.
- **Grammar & Tone Alert (`#D97706` / Soft Surface `#FFFBEB`):** Flags style revisions, passive voice, and phrasing warnings.
- **Spelling & Structural Errors (`#E11D48` / Soft Surface `#FFF1F2`):** Highlights critical spelling mistakes, broken links, or delivery errors.

## Typography

The typographic pairing balances contemporary human warmth with clinical screen readability. 

### Typographic Roles
- **Headlines & Interface Chrome (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Geometric, crisp numerals, and open apertures bring friendly sophistication to titles, modal headers, navigation, and KPI badges.
- **Editor & Long-form Drafting (Inter):** Highly legible, neutral, and meticulously spaced for continuous proofreading, email scanning, and side-by-side variation review.
- **Diagnostic Annotations:** Inline suggestions, grammar rule descriptions, and tone indicators utilize `body-sm` and `label-sm` with tight tracking for optimal density inside popovers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is built on a responsive 12-column grid utilizing strict 4px/8px modular spacing increments.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (≥1280px):** 12-column structure with `1.5rem` (24px) gutters, `3rem` margins, capped at a maximum width of `80rem` (1280px) for standard views, or dynamic 3-pane responsive canvas for the email studio (Sidebar: 280px, Editor: 1fr, Diagnostic Inspector: 360px).
- **Tablet (768px - 1279px):** 8-column layout with `1rem` (16px) gutters. The diagnostic inspector shifts to an off-canvas slideover or bottom expandable sheet.
- **Mobile (≤767px):** 4-column fluid layout with `1rem` margins. The navigation converts to an anchored bottom taskbar; toolbars collapse into segmented action pills above the virtual keyboard.

### Composition Principles
- Maintain generous whitespace (`2rem` - `3rem`) surrounding long-form compose viewports to eliminate claustrophobia.
- Dense micro-rhythms (`0.5rem` - `0.75rem`) are reserved for toolbar clusters, suggestion cards, and tone selector chips.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses ethereal, tinted ambient drop shadows combined with fine `1px` structural borders to convey layers without muddying the interface.

### The Elevation Stack
1. **Level 0 (Flat Canvas - `#FAFAFC`):** Background surface holding structural rails and app layout shells.
2. **Level 1 (Card & Section Surfaces - `#FFFFFF`):**
   - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
   - Shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`
   - Used for primary workspace canvas, template lists, and settled dashboard cards.
3. **Level 2 (Hover & Active Interactive Cards):**
   - Border: `1px solid #CBD5E1`
   - Shadow: `0 4px 6px -1px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.05), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)`
   - Used for selectable email templates, hovered variation cards, and active prompt presets.
4. **Level 3 (Contextual Popovers & Dropdowns):**
   - Border: `1px solid #E2E8F0`
   - Shadow: `0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.04)`
   - Used for inline grammar correction tooltips, tone switchers, and action menus.
5. **Level 4 (Modals & Overlays):**
   - Backdrop: `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)` with `backdrop-filter: blur(4px)`
   - Shadow: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`
   - Used for bulk generation wizards and platform settings.

## Shapes

The design system adopts a modern rounded aesthetic with strategic scale shifts:

- **Base Radius (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to form inputs, small action buttons, table rows, and toolbars.
- **Card Radius (`rounded-2xl` / `1rem` / 16px):** Standard envelope for email editor cards, template previews, modal containers, and dashboard tiles.
- **Pill Radius (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status badges, inline grammar error indicators, tone selector chips, and primary floating action buttons.
- **Underline Indicators:** Grammar and spelling markers inside text utilize gentle wavy or solid 2px underlines with rounded terminal caps, hovering `2px` below baseline text.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background in `#2563EB` with text `#FFFFFF`, subtle inner glow `inset 0 1px 0 rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.2)`. On hover: `#1D4ED8`, translated -1px on Y-axis.
- **AI Action ("Generate / Refine"):** Styled with the AI Spark Gradient (`#2563EB` to `#0284C7`), paired with an animated sparkle icon on hover.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, text `#0F172A`. On hover: background `#F8FAFC`, border `#CBD5E1`.
- **Tertiary / Ghost:** Transparent background, text `#475569`. On hover: background `#F1F5F9`, text `#0F172A`.

### Chips & Tone Selectors
- **Default:** Pill-shaped, background `#F1F5F9`, text `#475569`, border `1px solid transparent`.
- **Active / Selected:** Pill-shaped, background `#EFF6FF`, border `1px solid #BFDBFE`, text `#1D4ED8`, accompanied by a trailing active check or micro-dot.

### Text Inputs & Rich Text Canvas
- **Input Fields:** Surface `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, rounded `0.5rem`. Text `15px` Inter. On focus: border `#2563EB`, outer ring `3px rgba(37, 99, 235, 0.15)`.
- **Editor Surface:** Bound within a `rounded-2xl` card, border `1px solid #E2E8F0`, subtle shadow Level 1. Floating quick-action formatting bubble appears with a `rounded-lg` silhouette upon text selection.

### Inline Diagnostics & Grammar Badges
- **Spelling Correction:** Inline red squiggly or solid highlight `#FFE4E6` with `#E11D48` label. Hover brings up a popover with replacement options and an "Accept (Tab)" shortcut key indicator.
- **Grammar & Conciseness:** Amber highlight `#FEF3C7` with `#D97706` text tag. Displays before/after diffs side by side.
- **Clarity / Polished State:** Emerald micro-tag `#D1FAE5` with `#059669` icon confirming zero detected flaws.

### Checkboxes & Radio Controls
- **Checkboxes:** `18px x 18px`, `rounded-md`, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked state: `#2563EB` background with white checkmark icon.
- **Radio Buttons:** `18px x 18px`, circular, border `1.5px solid #CBD5E1`. Checked state: white base with an inner `#2563EB` solid core (`8px`).

### Cards & Generation Variations
- Standard card container uses `rounded-2xl`, `#FFFFFF` surface, `1px solid #E2E8F0`.
- **AI Alternative Card:** Stacked layout comparing original draft vs. generated versions with diff highlights. Header houses tone badge (e.g., "More Persuasive", "Casual") and quick one-click "Apply to Editor" action.
